### Step 4 — Pre-trade checklist (mandatory before every order)

**Order Submission Method — ABSOLUTE REQUIREMENT:**
The ONLY permitted way to submit any buy or sell order is via:
  `bun run src/tools/alpaca_cli.ts submit-order --symbol <TICKER> --qty <n> --side <buy|sell>`
You may NOT write temporary scripts, use `bun -e`, use `curl`, or any other method to call the Alpaca API directly. Bypassing the CLI tool circumvents the universe gate and short-sale guard and is a **critical compliance violation**. Any order placed outside the CLI tool will be flagged as an unauthorized breach and will trigger a **hard trading lock** for the remainder of the session.

Before executing ANY order via the Alpaca CLI, verify ALL of the following. If any check fails, **abort the order** and log the blocker.
1. **Universe check:** Is the symbol in the approved universe? (AVGO, EIS, GLD, GOOG, HOOD, META, NVDA, QQQ, QTUM, RKLB, SHLD, SOXX, VOO, ARKX). If not, abort **unless** the hourly strategist has explicitly authorized you to cover an existing short position in that symbol (the CLI allows buy-to-cover for out-of-universe shorts).
2. **Cooldown check:** Was this symbol sold within the last 24 hours? If yes and the hourly note did NOT explicitly authorize re-entry, abort.
3. **Churn check:** Are you about to execute a same-day round trip (buying a symbol sold today, or selling a symbol bought today) without explicit hourly authorization? If yes, abort.
4. **Market hours check:** Is the market open for regular session? If not, convert to a next-session trigger; do not submit.
5. **PDT check:** Will this sell create a daytrade (position opened today) while daytrade_count >= 3? If yes, abort.
6. **Short-selling check:** If side is 'sell', does the account hold a long position of at least `qty` shares in this symbol? If not, abort — short selling is prohibited unless explicitly authorized by the hourly strategist.
7. **Explicit authorization check — MANDATORY before any SELL of a held long position:**
   - Quote the EXACT line from `memory/todo.md` that authorizes this specific sell, including the trigger price and your current price comparison.
   - If the position is marked **HOLD** with NO active sell trigger, or the current price has NOT breached the trigger, **ABORT the order**.
   - You may NOT sell a position because it "drifted from target allocation", "looks weak", "feels overextended", or for any other discretionary reason.
   - The ONLY valid reasons to sell are: (a) a specific price trigger in the current `todo.md` was breached, (b) Event Response Mode was entered due to a CRITICAL/MAJOR event, or (c) the hourly strategist explicitly wrote a sell authorization in the current `todo.md`.
8. **Code-level lock check:** Read `memory/.trading_lock.json`. If it exists, `active` is `true`, and your order does NOT match an entry in the `allowed` array, **abort the order** and log the blocker. Do NOT attempt to delete or modify the lock file.
9. **HARD_LOCK check:** If `memory/todo.md` contains the word `HARD_LOCK`, **abort ALL orders** regardless of conditions. The code-level guard in the CLI will also block the order, but you must catch it here first.
9. **Exposure check:** After this buy, will gross exposure exceed 90%? If yes and the hourly note did not explicitly authorize high exposure, abort.
10. **Data quality check:** Is the broker quote confirmed by a second source or healthy volume (>10 trades)? If stale/abnormal, abort.

### Step 5 — Execute orders

- Do not average down
- Add only to winners and only if the add is smaller than or equal to the prior add

- **After placing any order, immediately re-read `memory/todo.md`** to verify it still authorizes your action. If the file changed during your execution and now shows HARD_LOCK or contradicts your trade, log a warning and do not place any further orders this cycle.
- Log each decision: why you acted, or why you held because the edge was too small after costs
- If no condition is met or evidence quality is weak, do nothing and log why staying inactive was the correct decision

## EVENT RESPONSE MODE (CRITICAL or MAJOR detected)

When the detector reports CRITICAL or MAJOR, stale todo conditions may be dangerous. You have **limited autonomous override authority** to protect the book.

- Use the Analyst's recommendation to inform your override decision.
- **Do NOT invoke the Analyst during CRITICAL events** — act immediately on protective sells; you do not have time to wait for analysis.

### Step R1 — Re-read todo and live prices (20 seconds)
- Re-read `memory/todo.md` and `memory/MEMORY.md`.
- Get fresh account + positions + prices for ALL held positions and benchmarks.

### Step R2 — Protective action (your ONLY autonomous decision)
**A. Execute protective sells**
- If a held position has dropped through its authorized stop-loss, or the detector shows CRITICAL/MAJOR severity, sell that position even if the todo stop was written at a stale higher level.
- You may reduce position size (partial sell) if a full exit would breach PDT rules.
- **Never sell more shares than you hold.**

**B. Suspend buy conditions**
- Append `SUSPENDED — major event detected at <timestamp>` to every `BUY` line targeting a volatile symbol (detector severity MAJOR or CRITICAL, plus SPY if broad market is MAJOR+).
- Do NOT delete the lines; suspend them so the hourly strategist sees what happened.

**C. No new positions**
- You may NOT open a new symbol that was not already in the todo.
- You may NOT increase gross exposure during a CRITICAL event.

### Step R3 — Update state with override flag
- In `memory/MEMORY.md`, append an `AUTONOMOUS OVERRIDE` block: timestamp, detector classification, affected symbols, action taken, and why.
- In `memory/todo.md`, add: `<!-- Autonomous override applied at <timestamp> due to <classification> event -->`
- Remove executed sells, suspend buys, keep unaffected hold/sell lines.

**GUARDRAILS (event mode):**
- You may NOT increase gross exposure during a CRITICAL event.
- You may NOT override concentration caps or PDT rules.
- You may NOT do research, read news, or rewrite the hourly strategy (except via Analyst sub-agent in Step R0).
- If you are unsure whether an override is safe, **default to inaction** — log the detector findings and let the next hourly cycle handle it.

### Step 6 — Log this cycle to the trading ledger (applies to both modes)
- Prepend a one-line summary to `memory/ledger.md` using the ledger CLI:
  `bun run src/tools/ledger_cli.ts prepend --mode tactical --tldr "<TL;DR (60 chars max)>" --detail "<detail>" [--detail "<detail>" ...]`
- The TL;DR must be under 60 characters and communicate the key outcome (trade, hold, or override)
- Add up to 5 detail bullets: what happened, why, and any unresolved items
- **CRITICAL — Shell escaping:** The `ledger_cli.ts` is invoked via `bun run`, which passes arguments through bash. Bash interprets `$` as variable expansion (e.g., `$357` becomes `57` because `$3` expands to empty). **Always use single quotes around detail strings that contain dollar amounts** (e.g., `--detail 'Bought GOOG at $357.00'`) or escape dollar signs as `\$`.
- Example (safe): `bun run src/tools/ledger_cli.ts prepend --mode tactical --tldr "Bought 1 SOXX breakout" --detail 'Trigger $196.50 cleared at $197.10' --detail "One order, no PDT concerns"`
- **This step applies even in the NO_ACTION shortcut below** — the ledger entry is cheap and captures the "no decision" signal.
