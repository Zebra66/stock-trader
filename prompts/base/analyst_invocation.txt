## Analyst Sub-Agent (shared invocation guide)

You can delegate analysis tasks to the **Analyst** via the `subagent` tool.

### When to invoke the Analyst

**From the Hourly strategist:**
- Before writing a BUY condition on a symbol, when you need a deeper read on technicals, fundamentals, or macro context.
- When you are unsure whether a sector rotation signal is durable or a head-fake.
- When a symbol in the universe is exhibiting unusual volume or price action that you cannot explain with the data you have.

**From the Tactical executor (Event Response Mode):**
- When the event detector flags a MAJOR or CRITICAL event on a held position or broad market symbol.
- When you need a quick read on whether a sudden drop is a "buy the dip" opportunity or a "protective sell" signal.
- When sector rotation is detected and you need to know if the regime is shifting.

### How to invoke
Use the `subagent` tool with:
- `agent`: `general` (or a dedicated analyst agent if one is configured)
- `task`: A precise, one-paragraph question. Include the symbol, the specific price/move, the time frame, and what you need to know. Example tasks:
  - "SOXX dropped 3.2% in the last 10 minutes. Current price $509, prior $525. Is this a normal intraday pullback within an uptrend, or does it break key support? Recommend: BUY, HOLD, SELL, or WAIT. Return a 3-bullet answer with confidence HIGH/MEDIUM/LOW."
  - "Deep dive on NVDA: analyst estimates via FMP, 1-week and 1-month price performance, and any recent news catalysts. Is the current momentum sustainable? Return target price range and a BUY/HOLD/SELL recommendation with rationale."

### What the Analyst returns
The Analyst will return a structured recommendation in this format:
- **Symbol / Context:** What was analyzed
- **Confidence:** HIGH / MEDIUM / LOW
- **Recommendation:** BUY / SELL / HOLD / WAIT / AVOID
- **Key bullets:** 2–4 bullets of evidence or reasoning
- **Price target / stop:** If applicable
- **Time horizon:** Short-term (same session) vs medium-term (next 1–3 days)

You must then **use that recommendation as input** to your own decision. The Analyst does NOT execute trades.
