### Step 3 — Analyse and decide (3 minutes)
- **Goal check:** Are we on track? If not, what is the dominant cause? Choose ONE: cash drag, bad symbol selection, late entries, excessive turnover, or macro regime mismatch.
- **Posture:** Classify as defensive, neutral, or offensive catch-up. Default to offensive catch-up if cash drag is the dominant issue AND the market is not in free-fall.
- **Cash deployment:** If gross exposure is below 60% and the market is open with normal liquidity, you MUST explain how new capital will be deployed in the next 1–2 hours. Do not stop at "stay selective."
- **Concentration limits:** QQQ max 45% of equity. Any single stock max 15% of equity. Any ETF (non-QQQ) max 20% of equity.
- **Cap-aware sizing:** Before writing any `BUY` line, recompute the post-fill allocation from live broker equity and current holdings. If that buy would breach a concentration cap after execution, reduce the share count or remove the order.
- **Cumulative queued-buy check:** Recompute the combined effect of ALL open `BUY` lines you write this cycle. Do not let the full set of queued buys push total gross exposure above your stated target band or consume more cash than is currently available unless you explicitly justify intentional margin usage in `MEMORY.md`.
- **PDT awareness:** If `daytrade_count >= 3`, or Alpaca already rejected a same-day closing order today, do not write intraday `SELL` lines for positions opened today. Treat those as next-session risk items and document the blocker in `MEMORY.md`.
- **Winners vs losers:** Trim losers that are down >5% with no near-term catalyst. Let winners run unless they exceed concentration caps.
- **Do not auto-take-profit healthy core winners:** Only write `SELL <TICKER> <N> shares at market if price >= $X` for a winner when it is above a concentration cap, has a near-term adverse catalyst, or is clearly climactic/extended. Do not harvest small gains from leaders just because they are green.
- **Set SIMPLE buy/sell conditions** in `memory/todo.md`. One line per condition:
  - `BUY <TICKER> <N> shares at market if price <= $X` (for dip buys)
  - `BUY <TICKER> <N> shares at market if price >= $Y` (for breakouts)
  - `SELL <TICKER> <N> shares at market if price <= $X` (stop loss)
  - `SELL <TICKER> <N> shares at market if price >= $Y` (take profit)
  - `HOLD <TICKER> — target allocation Z%`
- **Trigger freshness:** If a breakout `BUY ... if price >= $Y` line would already be in-the-money at the live price you are looking at, either raise the trigger modestly above the current price or explicitly state in `MEMORY.md` that it is an intentional immediate next-cycle buy. Do not leave stale breakout levels below the current price without explanation.
- **No-trade conditions:** Only write these when the market is closed, a data blackout exists, or a specific symbol has a known blocker (e.g. earnings in 30 min). Do not write generic "no-trade" rules.
